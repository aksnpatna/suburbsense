> Last updated: 2026-08-22

## Approach: Disclaimer-Based (Not Lawyer-Dependent)

**You do NOT need a lawyer or bank approval to launch.** The model is:
- **Suburb data** = public government data (ABS, ACARA, GTFS) → just attribute sources
- **Calculators** = rough estimates based on public rates → add "estimate only" disclaimer
- **Energy comparison** = links directly to CIMET (their terms govern) → add affiliate disclosure
- **NBN lookup** = public nbn™ API → no restrictions

GoodSuburb operates this way — they use disclaimers, not lawyer-drafted terms.

---

## What Needs No Legal Review

### AER Energy Data Display
- AER CDR API is open data, no accreditation required
- Free to display with attribution: "AER Energy Product Reference Data"
- No restrictions on showing plan rates

### Suburb Profiles (Public Data)
- ABS Census data: CC BY 4.0 — just attribute
- ACARA school data: CC BY 4.0 — just attribute
- GTFS transit data: CC BY 4.0 — just attribute
- OpenStreetMap: ODbL — just attribute

### Calculators
- Based on published government rates (State Revenue Offices, ATO, Housing Australia)
- Just add: "Rough estimate only. Actual amounts depend on your circumstances, lender assessment, and current rates."
- No bank approval needed — you're not providing credit advice

### NBN Lookup
- Uses nbn™ public API via nbnpy
- No terms restrict displaying results
